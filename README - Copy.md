## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a **Senior Software Engineer** with **7+ years** of hands-on experience designing and delivering **enterprise-grade ERP systems** for Bangladesh's garment, textile, dyeing & printing, and leather manufacturing industries. I turn complex factory-floor workflows into synchronized, scalable digital platforms — **HRMS, Payroll, Production, Finance, Costing, Commercial, SOP, ESS,** and **Intranet Portal** solutions.

My core foundation is the **.NET ecosystem and SQL Server**, and I'm actively expanding into **modern full-stack JavaScript** to build the next generation of cloud-native enterprise applications.

- 🔭 &nbsp;Currently a **Senior Software Engineer at Ventura Leatherwear Mfy (BD) Ltd.**, building **HRMS, Production & Finance ERP** on .NET Core and PHP stacks
- 🌱 &nbsp;Currently learning and expanding into **Next.js · Node.js · MongoDB** for modern full-stack development
- 🏗️ &nbsp;Working with **Clean Architecture, CQRS, Microservices, MediatR** and **Autofac** for modular, decoupled systems
- 📊 &nbsp;Building data-driven dashboards and reports with **Power BI, SSRS/RDLC, Chart.js** and **ECharts**
- 💬 &nbsp;Ask me about **ASP.NET Core, C#, SQL Server, ERP architecture** and database performance tuning
- 📍 &nbsp;Based in **Rangpur, Bangladesh** &nbsp;·&nbsp; 📫 &nbsp;Reach me at **mdalaminmiah4625@gmail.com**
- ⚡ &nbsp;Fun fact: I read Japanese **Hiragana & Katakana**
